<objective>
Close COSMETIC-03: replace the entity-nbsp-bound range fragment in the checkup.html hero H1 with a Tailwind `whitespace-nowrap` span around the entire phrase "за 1–2 дня", so the numeric range never splits mid-phrase across any viewport width and the en-dash typography remains correct.

Purpose: The current fragment uses three `&nbsp;` entities to glue "за", "1", "–", "2", and "дня" together. This works at most widths but the v3.1 Phase 38.1 Playwright UX validation found a viewport where the line wrapped between "за 1–" and "2 дня" — the nbsp glue was insufficient because the en-dash itself is a wrap opportunity for some browsers. Switching to a single `whitespace-nowrap` span around the whole phrase removes the ambiguity: either the entire phrase fits on one line, or it wraps as a unit to the next line. It can never split mid-range.

Output: checkup.html line 138 fragment replaced per locked decision D-05. Phase 39-03 byte-identity gate passes. Playwright MCP measurement at 6 viewports confirms the phrase always renders as a single line-height unit.
</objective>

- The em-dash `&mdash;` (U+2014) preceded by an ASCII space remains OUTSIDE the new inner span — it is a sentence-level separator between "Severance Hospital" and the time-frame phrase, NOT part of the range itself. - A new inner `<span class="whitespace-nowrap">` wraps the phrase "за 1–2 дня".
- The en-dash entity `&ndash;` (U+2013) is **preserved verbatim** — it is the canonical Russian typographic range character. Do NOT replace with `-`, `&minus;`, `&mdash;`, or any other character.
- The two entity-level `&nbsp;` characters that were INSIDE the old fragment ("за&nbsp;1" and "2&nbsp;дня") are **removed** — they become regular ASCII spaces. The Tailwind `whitespace-nowrap` class on the wrapping span enforces non-breaking behavior on the entire content, so the entity glue is no longer needed (it would be redundant).
- The closing `</span>` for the inner nowrap span sits BEFORE the closing `</span>` for the outer text-mu-text-900 span.
