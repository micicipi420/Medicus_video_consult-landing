<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Checkup H1 (UIREV-NEW-01) — approach

**Chosen approach: Split the brand-name gradient span into two adjacent spans at the "и" connector.**

The overflow is caused by `bg-clip-text` forcing the span "Samsung Medical Center и Severance Hospital" to compute its intrinsic content width on a single unbreakable line, because a gradient must paint a contiguous box to be clipped. At `text-7xl font-extrabold` the span measures **717.67px** inside a **592px** parent. Splitting at "и" gives two shorter spans, each of which can wrap independently and participates in normal inline layout.

Rejected alternatives:
- **Drop `bg-clip-text` entirely** — kills the two-color hospital-name accent that is a deliberate design choice across all service pages
- **Reduce H1 size to `lg:text-6xl`** — solves the symptom but leaves the gradient-span fragility lurking for any future hospital name added to the phrase
- **`white-space: normal` + explicit `<br>`** — introduces a desktop-only newline that makes mobile layout awkward

Split-at-"и" is the least-invasive change that addresses the actual cause.

### Index country-cards (UIREV-NEW-02) — approach

**Chosen approach: Inspect the cards' grid wrapper and add the missing responsive breakpoint.**

9 cards at 799px on a 360px viewport means either (a) a `grid-cols-N` without `md:`/`lg:` reset that fixes column count across viewports, or (b) card children with `min-width` that forces the cards to their largest content. Needs a grep for `bg-white/60 backdrop-blur-2xl rounded-[2.5rem]` plus the nearest grid ancestor. The fix is to add `grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3` (or equivalent) so mobile gets 1 col.

### Other overflows (UIREV-NEW-03) — approach

**Chosen approach: Root-cause each page with Playwright introspection, not blanket fixes.**

- `treatment-abroad.html` (63px): likely the stat bar (rework was in Phase 34 scope) or the hero image aspect ratio on mobile
- `404.html` (66px): H1 "Страница не найдена" at `text-5xl` — likely needs `break-words` or reduced size on `sm`
- `contacts.html` (14px): small enough to be a stray `px-1` margin or a `w-[420px]` somewhere — single grep + edit

Do NOT paper over with `overflow-x: hidden` on body — that hides the symptom without fixing the cause and breaks position:sticky descendants.

### SVG rx syntax (UIREV-NEW-04) — approach

`<rect rx="0 0 3 3">` is invalid — SVG `rx` takes one length. The original intent was "flat top, rounded bottom" (a tab-style rectangle). Replace with `<path d="..."` using `M … H … V … A rx,ry ...`  or just drop the per-corner distinction and use `rx="3"` if the visual impact is negligible. Inspect the SVG first — if it's an icon rendered at 16-24px, a single rx value is probably visually equivalent.

### Re-validation

After all fixes land: re-run the Playwright script at **320, 375, 768, 1024, 1440** (5 viewports × 6 pages = 30 screenshots). Drop 1920 — the 1440 check is representative enough for RHYTHM-10. Confirm `scrollWidth === clientWidth` on every page at every viewport. Append results to the UX validation doc.
