  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Open index.html in a local server or directly in browser. Perform these checks in order:

**Check 1 — Hero gradient mesh (GLASS-01):**
- Load the page in light mode
- The hero section background should have a subtle cool-tinted depth — barely perceptible blue/teal gradient shapes against the white base
- The gradient must NOT overpower the hero text or CTA buttons — it is a whisper of color, not a colored background
- In dark mode (toggle the theme switch), the gradient tints should still be subtly visible over the navy (#0F1923) background

**Check 2 — Header glass on scroll (GLASS-02):**
- Scroll down past the hero section in light mode
- The sticky header should become frosted/translucent — you should see page content blurred through the header background
- The header should NOT be solid white after scrolling — it must show the glass effect
- Scroll back to top — header returns to solid white (non-scrolled state)
- Toggle to dark mode and scroll — header must be opaque dark navy (#0F1923), NOT showing any glass/blur effect

**Check 3 — Pricing card glass (GLASS-03):**
- Scroll to the Pricing (Стоимость) section
- The pricing section background should be a light blue-to-green gradient (NOT solid white)
- The pricing card (.pricing__card) should appear frosted/translucent over that gradient — you should see the gradient colors slightly through the card background
- In dark mode, the pricing section should have a dark blue-navy gradient and the card should be a solid opaque dark surface (rgba(30,44,58,0.95)) — no glass blur

**Check 4 — @supports fallback (GLASS-04):**
In Chrome DevTools, disable backdrop-filter:
- Open DevTools → Console → paste: document.querySelector('.site-header').style.backdropFilter = 'none'; document.querySelector('.pricing__card').style.backdropFilter = 'none';
- With glass disabled, both the header (scrolled state) and pricing card must show as solid opaque — white header, white/light pricing card — NO murky translucent appearance
- The @supports fallback ensures this automatically in real unsupported browsers

**Check 5 — MANDATORY 4x CPU throttle performance test (GLASS-04 exit criterion):**
This check is REQUIRED before the phase can be marked complete.
1. Open Chrome DevTools → Performance tab
2. Set CPU throttle to 4x slowdown
3. Start recording
4. Scroll slowly through the entire page, spending ~3 seconds at the pricing section where both header + card glass are simultaneously visible
5. Stop recording
6. Inspect the FPS chart — look for the green frames-per-second line
7. PASS: FPS stays at or above 50fps throughout scroll
8. FAIL: If FPS drops below 50fps during the pricing section scroll:
   - First remediation: change `--glass-blur: blur(12px)` to `blur(8px)` in :root of css/styles.css, retest
   - Second remediation: if still failing, remove `card--glass` class from the pricing card in index.html and keep only the header glass

**Check 6 — Full visual regression at 390px and 1440px:**
Using DevTools responsive mode:
- Set to 390px width: verify all 11 sections look correct, hero text is readable, pricing card is contained within bounds, no overflow
- Set to 1440px width: verify hero two-column layout, pricing card centered, glass effects visible
- In both viewports: verify the form section is unaffected by glass CSS — form fields, submit button, and validation states are normal

**Check 7 — Form submission flow:**
Open the consultation form (click the CTA button). Fill in: Name, Phone (+7...), Specialization (select any option). Submit the form. The form should submit normally — glass modifier on the pricing card must NOT have affected the form wrapper, input styles, or lead-form section background.
