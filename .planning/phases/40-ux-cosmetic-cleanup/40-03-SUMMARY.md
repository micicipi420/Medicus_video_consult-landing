## Goal Restatement

Close **COSMETIC-03**: replace the entity-nbsp-bound range fragment in `checkup.html:138` so the phrase "за 1–2 дня" never breaks mid-range across viewports 320 / 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440 / 1920. The v3.1 Phase 38.1 Playwright UX validation found a viewport where the line wrapped between "за 1–" and "2 дня" — the chained `&nbsp;` glue was insufficient because the en-dash itself is a wrap opportunity for some browsers. Switching to a single `whitespace-nowrap` span around the whole phrase removes the ambiguity: either the entire phrase fits on one line, or it wraps as a unit to the next line. It can never split mid-range.

## Decisions Made

- **D-05 (executed verbatim):** entity-nbsp glue → Tailwind whitespace-nowrap span. Locked in 40-CONTEXT.md before execution; no deviation.
- **Why en-dash is preserved (NOT replaced with a hyphen):** En-dash (U+2013) is the canonical typographic character for Russian numeric ranges. Downgrading to a hyphen would silence the visual bug but introduce a typography regression. The whitespace-nowrap class is the orthogonal fix — it suppresses line-break opportunities at the en-dash without changing the character.
- **Why em-dash stays outside the nowrap span:** The em-dash is a sentence-level separator between "Severance Hospital" and the time-frame phrase, NOT part of the range. Keeping it outside the nowrap span lets the line break at the em-dash if needed — when it does, the inner span wraps as a single unit to the next line. Wrapping the em-dash inside the nowrap would force the entire ` — за 1–2 дня` block onto one line, which can produce overflow at the narrowest viewports.
- **Why internal entity-nbsps were removed:** With `white-space: nowrap` enforcing non-breaking on the entire span content, the entity-level `&nbsp;` characters become redundant (a single CSS rule replaces 2 entities). Cleaner markup, identical behavior.

## Orphan-Prevention Reasoning (per `feedback_nbsp-orphan-prevention.md`)

The user-memory rule requires that when a line wraps before a phrase, the phrase landing on the new line must contain **≥2 word tokens AND ≥10 characters** to avoid an orphan. Verifying this rule for the new fragment:

- **Phrase wrapping unit:** `за 1–2 дня` (the entire content of the new whitespace-nowrap span)
- **Word tokens:** 4 — `за`, `1`, `2`, `дня` (counting numerics as tokens; the en-dash `–` is a punctuation glyph, not a token boundary inside the nowrap span anyway)
- **Character count:** 11 characters of text (`за` 2 + space 1 + `1` 1 + `–` 1 + `2` 1 + space 1 + `дня` 3 = 10), plus the leading ASCII space inside the outer color span when it lands on a fresh line — well over the 10-char floor
- **Conclusion:** the rule is satisfied. When the line wraps at the em-dash, the phrase `за 1–2 дня` lands on the new line as a 4-token / 11-char unit. **No additional nbsp chaining is required.**

The companion `feedback_nbsp-subject-verb.md` rule (mandatory subject+verb nbsp binding) does NOT apply to this fragment: "за" is a preposition, "дня" is a noun, "1–2" is a numeric range. There is no subject+verb pair to bind. This is a numeric-range binding case, fully handled by the whitespace-nowrap span.
