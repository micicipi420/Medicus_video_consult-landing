**Filter definitions (exact values):**

1. **#liquid-refract-sm** (small elements: badges, nav buttons, footer icons)
   - Purpose: Zero/minimal refraction -- small elements look noisy with displacement
   - feTurbulence: baseFrequency="0.02" numOctaves="1" seed="92"
   - feGaussianBlur: stdDeviation="1"
   - feDisplacementMap: scale="0"
   - Rationale: scale=0 means no visible displacement. The filter chain exists so the CSS pattern stays uniform (url(#id) blur(...)), but produces zero visual distortion. Higher baseFrequency with 1 octave = faster to compute = less GPU waste.

2. **#liquid-refract-md** (medium elements: cards ~300-400px)
   - Purpose: Baseline card refraction -- the "current experience" with slight refinement
   - feTurbulence: baseFrequency="0.012" numOctaves="2" seed="92"
   - feGaussianBlur: stdDeviation="2"
   - feDisplacementMap: scale="18"
   - Rationale: Reduced from scale=30 to scale=18 -- the current scale=30 is aggressive on cards and causes noticeable text wobble. baseFrequency bumped from 0.008 to 0.012 for tighter noise pattern at card scale. 2 octaves retained for natural fractal texture.

3. **#liquid-refract-lg** (large elements: stats-glass, hero sections)
   - Purpose: Subtle wide-area refraction -- reads as "glass" without distorting content behind
   - feTurbulence: baseFrequency="0.006" numOctaves="3" seed="92"
   - feGaussianBlur: stdDeviation="3"
   - feDisplacementMap: scale="12"
   - Rationale: Lower baseFrequency (0.006) produces larger, gentler waves appropriate for wide surfaces. 3 octaves adds subtle detail at multiple scales. scale=12 is conservative -- on a 1200px surface, 12px displacement is barely perceptible but adds glass-like shimmer. Higher stdDeviation=3 softens the noise further for legibility.
